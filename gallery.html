About the project

Who made
this site

This website is a student-led promotion project for Legazpi City, Albay — built to showcase the city's spots, culture, and cuisine to a wider audience online.

V1 is a static site - no backend, no database, just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Photos are gathered on the ground by the team across Brgy 16 Kawit, Brgy 25 Lapu-Lapu Street, and the boulevard etc.. Everything you see here is local, firsthand, and made with genuine pride for the city.
